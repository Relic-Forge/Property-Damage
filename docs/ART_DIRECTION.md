# Art Direction

## Style target

**Premium cartoon cutout destruction.**

Not pixel art. Not realism. Not flat clipart.

The look should feel like:

- clean cartoon shapes
- chunky silhouettes
- readable props
- bold outlines
- warm shadows
- funny object labels/details
- bright impact moments
- slightly adult garage humor without offensive jokes

## Tone

Smirk, not shock.

The comedy comes from ordinary objects behaving dramatically: amps crushing tables, cymbals bouncing where they should not, a fog machine causing an incident, and score labels treating dumb chaos like an insurance claim.

## Color direction

Garage background:

- dark plum
- dusty brown
- faded purple
- warm concrete gray
- dim amber light

Impact objects:

- neon yellow
- hot pink
- electric cyan
- bright orange
- metallic lavender

UI:

- dark translucent panels
- cream text
- yellow highlights
- pink/cyan accent moments

## Asset design rules

Each object needs a strong silhouette and one joke detail.

Examples:

- Bass amp: too many knobs, duct tape corner, tiny label reading “LOUD-ish”.
- Guitar: scratched body, one missing string, sticker that says “solo later”.
- Cymbal: too shiny, cracked edge, slightly ridiculous wobble.
- Mic stand: unnecessarily long and pointy.
- Fog machine: warning sticker, purple smoke ports, “probably fine” vibe.
- Old TV: thick glass, bent antenna, local-access energy.
- Folding table: visibly flimsy legs.
- Garage shelf: overloaded with cables and mystery boxes.

## Break-state pattern

Every important object should eventually have:

```txt
intact sprite
broken sprite/parts
small debris pieces
impact sound
optional bonus text
```

Avoid true fracture simulation in V1. Use pre-made broken parts.

## No-offense guardrail

Allowed:

- bad gear choices
- local-band jokes
- landlord/security-deposit jokes
- insurance math jokes
- harmless social embarrassment
- absurd object behavior

Avoid:

- real-world politics
- protected-class jokes
- gore/injury
- sexual jokes
- cruelty to people or animals
- addiction jokes
- punching down
